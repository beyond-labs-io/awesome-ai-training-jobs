# Contributing

Thanks for helping keep this list accurate.

## Adding a platform

Open a pull request that adds one row to the **Platforms** table. A platform qualifies when it:

- pays people to train, test, evaluate or label data for AI models;
- is a real, operating company with its own website;
- does not charge workers to apply, train or onboard.

Include a link to the platform's own site or careers page as evidence in the PR description. Use a neutral one-line description, no marketing language.

## Fixing details

Pay ranges, onboarding times and reliability ratings change. If something is out of date, open an issue with a source (the platform's own page, a help-center article or a pay statement with personal details removed).

## Not accepted

- Referral or affiliate links in pull requests
- Individual job postings (the **Latest open roles** table is generated automatically, so edits to it are overwritten)
- Platforms that charge workers fees
