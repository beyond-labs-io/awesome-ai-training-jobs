# Awesome AI Training Jobs [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of platforms that pay people to train, test and evaluate AI models, plus a daily-updated table of open remote roles.

AI training work means writing and grading model answers, red-teaming, labeling data and reviewing code for AI labs. It's mostly remote contract work, and pay ranges from a few dollars an hour for microtasks to $100+/hr for doctors, lawyers, engineers and PhDs.

This list is maintained by [AI Work Expert](https://www.aiworkexpert.com), which tracks these platforms and their open roles.

## Before you apply

- **Never pay to apply.** Real platforms don't charge application, training or onboarding fees.
- **Check the domain.** Apply only on the platform's own site; scammers copy platform names in emails and chats.
- **Expect an assessment.** Most platforms use a test or an AI interview before any paid work.
- **Work is project-based.** Hours can stop when a project ends, so don't count on it as steady income at first.
- **Check how you get paid** in your country before you start: [payout guide for India](https://www.aiworkexpert.com/blog/india-payouts).
